# One development falsification of cap persistence

Saved development records showed that predicted tracker memory can continue
to lower the additional distance-cap target after current pixel detections
stop. The diagnostic matched canonical road segments, forward direction and
first passage, with continuity and branch-ambiguity checks. It separated
reverse, repeated, recovery and contact intervals and rejected unreliable
whole intervals. Saved timing differences and cap exposure do not identify
the causal share of lap-time cost. Missing detection can be occlusion, so
tracker age does not establish that a hazard or its cap is unnecessary.

The single preregistered intervention passes only age-zero hazards to the
additional distance cap. The original tracker memory remains available to
near-hazard scheduling, lateral control, routing and steering. The radial cap
formula, constants and pixel-speed feedback remain identical. The inherited
controller is called once, and its steering history is preserved.

The fixed comparison uses a previously rescued completion first, followed
by distinct common completions. Existing results from the exact provisional
baseline version are reused without new simulation. Any official completion
loss stops the variant and all unlaunched cases; a resource or technical
censor is inconclusive. There is one variant, with no outcome-based retuning
or automatic retries. Keep criteria were fixed before new results and require
preserved completions and an observed aggregate development lap improvement.

The variant preserved the rescued completion and the preselected common
completions, with shorter observed laps and no observed collision damage in
these runs. It met the registered keep rule and is retained only as a separate
development-selected improvement candidate. There was no additional variant,
retuning, retry, holdout evaluation or sealed evaluation. This result does
not establish that every aged cap is unnecessary or that the same change
will preserve completion on unseen roads.

The formal champion and provisional baseline remain immutable. Any kept
variant is a separate development-selected candidate. This comparison does
not establish holdout performance, broad safety or completion probability.
No additional holdout or sealed evaluation is part of this investigation.
The application plan's final sealed confirmation is G5; it is not a current
G4 promotion prerequisite. Fresh-machine and exact official-runtime parity
remain unproved by local execution.
