# Same-time near-path diagnosis

`saved_near_path_diagnosis.py` uses a fixed window of an admitted saved trace. It compares the selected path and subsequent vehicle poses in the decision's hull-local frame, which is equivalent to transporting that path into world coordinates. Projection arclength is bounded by the previous projection and actual sample translation, with each candidate segment clipped to that contiguous interval. It records the segment, arclength and same-time body-to-tangent angle. The target-point bearing is a separate quantity. There are no global nearest-path branch jumps, new observations, policy calls or simulator steps.

A newly planned path starts at the current vehicle origin. Zero pre-action distance is therefore a construction, not evidence of successful tracking. Its first segment is a synthetic connection to the supported grid ahead of the vehicle; it does not establish observed road support beneath the body. Heading disagreement with that planned chord differs from disagreement with a distant target, but neither alone proves physical departure or abnormal tracking through a curve. Raster-grid kinks also do not specify a dynamically feasible smooth trajectory.

Policy targets, HUD speed, recorded action before the extra cap, final action and previous steering state belong to the decision time. Wheel-joint and hull responses belong to subsequent raw times. The recorded pre-cap action has already passed through the inherited steering clip and smoothing. Any reconstructed unclipped steering angle is labelled formula-derived rather than recorded. The extra cap can change gas and brake while leaving steering unchanged. Recorded fallback flags and cap flags are kept separate from an absent recovery-mode signal.

The limited window supports descriptions of angle, action and actuator timing. Clipping, changing path direction, speed differences or delayed wheel response do not by themselves identify which intervention would restore progress. A proposed intervention requires specific supporting evidence; otherwise the development-case diagnosis closes. Repeating the same saved episode under another metric name is not an automatic follow-up. Private cases, exact action indices, geometry and numeric outputs remain outside the repository.

In the admitted window, all bounded projections remained on the synthetic first connector. A near-connector heading gap existed separately from the farther target bearing, so the earlier marker was not solely a distant-target or timestamp artifact. The chosen connector changed direction across adjacent decisions while the wheel joint still reflected the previous command. These observations establish disagreement with local planned intent, without establishing departure from a supported near-vehicle path, normal curve feasibility or a uniquely failing component. No specific intervention was sufficiently supported; the saved development-case diagnosis closed.
